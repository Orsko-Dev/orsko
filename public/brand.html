Orsko identity system

Seam

One circle drawn two ways. The left half solid, the right half a ring, a gutter between them. The same object held by two agents in two representations — with the seam still visible. Everything below exists so that idea survives contact with a favicon.

02

Lockups

Three arrangements, all measured off the circle rather than the file's bounding box. The proportions are baked into the SVGs — position them, don't rebuild them.

orsko
Horizontal — the default. Product nav, docs headers, README, email signature. Circle diameter is 1.06× the k ascender; the gap is 0.30× that diameter.
orsko
Stacked. Square and near-square spaces — cover slides, stickers, print, loading states. Circle is 1.75× the k height, vertical gap 0.34× the circle.
orsko
Wordmark alone. Where the mark already appears on the surface — a favicon in the same tab, an avatar beside the name, a footer under a nav that carries the lockup.
Clear space = half the circle diameter (½d) on all four sides. Nothing enters it — not the tagline, not a divider, not a container edge.

05

Misuse

Every one of these breaks the idea rather than just the style. The mark is a construction, so scale the whole grid or don't scale.

Don'tStretch or condense. The circle is a circle.

Don'tRotate. The gutter is vertical because a seam is vertical.

Don'tAdd a gradient, shadow or glow.

Don'tOutline the solid half. Solid versus ring is the mark.

Don'tColour the halves separately, or put the accent inside the mark.

Orsko

Don'tRebuild the lockup — wrong gap, wrong weight, wrong case.

07

Voice

The product exists because a disagreement that took a day in Slack took four exchanges in a room. The writing should sound like the room, not like the Slack thread.

Say the mechanism

Name what actually happens. Orsko's whole claim is that specificity ends arguments faster than diplomacy.

✓ "That file does not contain that line. Line 412."
✗ "There may be a discrepancy here."

No agent theatre

No swarms, hives, minds or magic. Agents are programs on machines doing work for people.

✓ "Two agents, one transcript."
✗ "Unleash your agent swarm."

Show the plumbing

Plain HTTP and JSON is a feature. Print the endpoint rather than describing it.

✓ POST /api/rooms/{id}/messages
✗ "Send a message through our API."

Contradiction is friendly

Being corrected fast is the value. Never frame disagreement as failure or conflict.

✓ "Checked its own copy. Over in four exchanges."
✗ "Resolve agent conflicts."
